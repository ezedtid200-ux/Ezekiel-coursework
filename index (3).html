Under the hood

One system, built to run everywhere

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information EXetecth collects when you buy or sell on the platform, and how it is used and protected.

1. What we collect

  • Account details you provide: name, email address, phone number, and a securely hashed password.
  • Order information: items purchased, delivery address, and order status history.
  • Seller information: product listings, stock levels, and incoming order records.

2. How we use it

  • To create and manage your buyer or seller account and keep you signed in securely.
  • To process orders, update stock, and let sellers fulfil and track deliveries.
  • To contact you about an order, a listing, or a support request you raised.

3. Price comparison links

The "Compare on Jumia / Konga / Jiji / Temu" buttons open that platform's own public search page in a new tab. EXetecth does not send your personal data to these platforms and does not scrape or store data from them — it simply builds a search link.

4. Data storage & security

Passwords are stored using salted hashing, never in plain text. Data is kept only for as long as needed to run your account and fulfil orders.

5. Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to close your account and erase your data, by contacting us using the details below.

6. Contact

Return & Refund Policy

Because EXetecth is a multi-vendor marketplace, each order is fulfilled by the individual seller — this policy sets the minimum standard every seller on the platform must honour.

1. Return window

  • Buyers may request a return within 7 days of the order being marked "Delivered".
  • Items must be unused, in original packaging, and with all accessories included.
  • Items marked as final sale, or personal-hygiene items such as earbuds once unsealed, cannot be returned unless faulty.

2. Faulty or wrong items

If an item arrives damaged, faulty, or different from what was listed, you can request a full refund or a free replacement — no restocking fee applies.

3. How to start a return

  • Open "Order history" on your account and select "Request a return" on the order.
  • Or contact the seller directly, or reach EXetecth support on 0703 184 7102 / 0802 383 3702.

4. Refunds

Once the seller confirms the returned item, refunds are issued to the original payment method within 3–7 business days. Delivery fees are refunded only when the return is due to a seller error.

5. Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled free of charge while status is still "Pending". Once a seller marks an order "Processing" or later, cancellation is treated as a return.
